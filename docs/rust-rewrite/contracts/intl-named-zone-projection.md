# Named-zone Custom projection

`NamedTimeZoneDataImage::for_custom_projection` resolves requested names through the original full IANA catalogue, canonicalizes them to primary identifiers, rejects duplicate aliases, and includes UTC. The resulting image retains every original TZif record associated with each selected primary, including its aliases. It retains the full identity catalogue and country membership on the same native owner. Fixed offsets continue through the original offset algebra.

The private projected descriptor binds the Custom identity and full pinned payload. Admission derives the complete expected selected frame and compares every byte before constructing the native owner. TZif parsing, certified gap topology, offset selection, inverse local-time queries, and next/previous transition selection use the original algorithms. Full Minimal, Conformance, and unfiltered named Custom images keep their original bytes.

Full identity lookup and `Intl.supportedValuesOf('timeZone')` remain available independently of selected transition records. A known omitted name returns `UnavailableIdentifier`, `UnavailableNamedIdentifier`, or `UnavailableTimeZone` at the relevant native boundary, displayed as `named time zone "Europe/Paris" is unavailable in the selected Custom data`. Unknown identifiers retain their original unknown-name error. Omitted names never acquire UTC transitions or an offset fallback.

DateTime and TimeZoneNames producers consume the actual selected IANA owner. They physically remove unrelated localized zone and metazone names, retaining original patterns and full small geography, country, and preferred-zone authority. Exact descriptors bind the selected Named digest and complete original native source. DateTime locale, calendar, currency-independent numbering, and named-zone projections compose through the same native representation. Plans validate transition availability before publication and retain the actual owner through portable decoding and formatting.

Native controls cover canonical aliases, original TZif byte closure, full identity/country lifetime, DST and historical/future offset/inverse/transition parity, all six name styles, selected scalar/range DateTime output, calendar/numbering composition, known omitted errors, foreign foundations, and checksum-consistent descriptor or native-row damage. Shared SDK/manifest/cache/CLI controls consume actual selected artifacts. These controls are authored and have not been executed in the current source batch.
