# Ordinary Generator ForIn source ownership

An ordinary Generator ForIn in its complete FunctionBody region has one checked
source owner for its target, advance state, original selected-key
initialization, complete body and exit. The common carrier selects the
Generator protocol and retains its existing inclusive region validator. The
initialization has its own range between advance and body;
Continue returns to advance, while Break exits the entire owner. The constructor
consumes the actual source suspension tape and original head proof, including
the AST identity and original lexical source-to-storage names. The source plan
and lowerer consume the same ranges even for a loop without Yield.

Four distinct original invocation allocations retain the completed raw target,
the private native enumeration edge, selected String key and iteration-result
value. The completed head publishes the raw target before the original head TDZ
record is left; native boxing and cursor construction follow that publication.
The head's TDZ record and actual per-iteration record remain the original
analyzed environments. An uncaptured lexical binding stays in its analyzed
activation cell; a captured binding stays in its actual iteration environment.
A genuine source Block has its separate nested lexical record.

The ordinary eager and complete lowerers share
`lowering/for_in/head.rs`. Its original Identifier, Var, Let, Const, member,
private-member and Array/Object pattern operations remain the sole per-key
semantic authority. The complete owner supplies the retained String key to
those operations; the body resumes after that initialization. It never selects
the previous key or evaluates its Reference/default/pattern again. Enumeration
traps invalidate unknown user-code facts before body lowering. Original eager
nullish-head effects, parameter widening and Annex B initializer behavior are
retained. The WebCompat call-target facility keeps its actual eager throwing
path and adds no phase states.

The native cursor shares the original OwnPropertyKeys, current own-descriptor,
visited-name and prototype algorithm. Symbols are excluded. A missing own
descriptor does not enter the visited set; a present nonenumerable descriptor
does. Prototype keys are acquired only after the current snapshot is drained.
The cursor is private native GC storage, with no public iterator, `return`
lookup or IteratorClose for ForIn. Array binding/assignment heads still use
their original separate IteratorClose authority before the body begins.

The real analyzed continuation token selects this owner only for an ordinary
Generator's complete FunctionBody region. A foreign ForOf body keeps its
existing iterator continuation and suppresses inherited complete-owner depths.
Nested callable bodies have their own analysis. Yield/Await in per-key
References, patterns and Annex B initializers are owned by their actual
complete ranges. Plain Async and AsyncGenerator use their corresponding
checked execution tags on the same carrier. Unsupported foreign iterator
composition retains its separate boundary. Complete target expressions and the
existing admitted
classic-loop, If, Switch, With, Try/finally and nested ForIn body owners are
consumed without a second graph or interpreter.

The former per-protocol carrier and scalar source facade are
[retired under T02](obsolete-for-in-carrier-removal.md). Their original private
control names and source strings remain mounted under the common carrier.

Persistent iteration-result completion uses the common native statement-list
authority and actual recursive `EmptyStatementCompletionIr` source proof.
Generated declaration prefixes do not overwrite the preceding nonempty
completion, while a bare source Yield publishes its real resumed Value.
Yield retains the cursor and original records; outward completion uses the
existing whole-completion/finalizer cleanup before retiring private edges.

Source controls cover complete target/body suspensions, original captured
Const/pattern cells, eager enclosing phase owners and foreign-head refusals.
Paired strict/sloppy Wasm controls observe lazy Proxy traces, descriptor
shadowing/deletion, primitive/nullish targets, interleaved GC, original
References/patterns, IteratorClose, head TDZ and labelled/injected completions.
A separate sloppy control observes original With record deletion and global
Reference fallback. Existing eager enumeration controls are retained.

This batch is source-only. These controls and the native joins require the
single coordinated compilation and focused/broad verification checkpoint;
their authorship is not a passing execution result.
